About me

Connecting engineering research with practical impact.

An academic profile focused on research, teaching, interdisciplinary collaboration, and the development of sustainable engineering solutions.

Academic profile

I am a Mechanical Engineering academic at K. R. Mangalam University, Gurugram. My interests include heat transfer, thermal management, solar thermal systems, phase-change materials, and sustainable materials and processes.

I also contribute to research facilitation and instrumentation-facility activities, supporting researchers with research planning, characterization, and academic development.

Solar Thermal Systems

Artificial roughness, rib and baffle geometries, heat-transfer enhancement, exergy analysis, and thermo-hydraulic performance of solar air heaters.

◈

Thermal Management

Microchannel and stepped micro-pin-fin heat sinks, flow arrangements, conjugate heat transfer, and cooling strategies for high-heat-flux systems.

❄

Phase-Change Materials

Composite PCMs, latent heat thermal energy storage, ice-slurry systems, solar refrigeration, and thermal-energy applications.

⌁

CFD & Numerical Modelling

Computational fluid dynamics, turbulence modelling, mesh independence, finite-volume methods, and simulation-based design evaluation.

♧

Biochar & Sustainable Materials

Biomass-derived biochar, material characterization, thermal properties, and potential applications in energy and environmental systems.

◎

Sustainable Construction

Geopolymer concrete, recycled aggregates, industrial by-products, and cradle-to-site life-cycle assessment.
